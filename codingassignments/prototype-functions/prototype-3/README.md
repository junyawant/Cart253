## Description

Another useful tool! Skip Counts is the prototype I've made in order to aid me as I tutor my 5-6th grade math students. Every thursday afternoon, right before the timed multiplications (prototype 1), we do skip counts! 

How its useful: Each class my students have to multiply 3 random numbers (normally of my own choice) (10 products of each number)

## How it Works
- Use the LMB and click anywhere on the canvas to generate 3 new numbers!
